Sample Crop Disease Information

Example: Tomato Early Blight

Early blight is a common fungal disease that can affect tomato plants.

Common Symptoms:
  • Brown spots on older leaves.
  • Yellowing around affected areas.
  • Leaves may gradually dry and fall.
Possible Causes:
  • Fungal infection.
  • Prolonged leaf moisture.
  • Warm and humid environmental conditions.
General Advisory Suggestions:
  • Remove affected leaves carefully.
  • Avoid unnecessary water accumulation around plants.
  • Water near the soil instead of wetting leaves.
  • Keep sufficient space between plants for airflow.
  • Consult an agricultural expert for appropriate treatment.
Note: This is sample educational information. CropCare does not currently identify diseases using AI. Actual disease diagnosis requires expert verification.
